Lokari Africa

Kenya’s trusted marketplace for local commerce.

Think of the best mechanic in your town. The mama mboga everyone trusts. The salon that never lets you down. Most of them don’t exist online, and that has always bothered me. So I built Lokari. Every business is checked by a real person before it goes live, listing is free, and customers finally have one place they can trust.

  • 57towns covered
  • 100%human-verified listings
  • Freeto list, free to stay
  • Smart business directory
  • Exclusive local deals
  • Community events
  • Verified customer reviews
  • Quotes & Ask Lokari
  • Reels

For businesses

  1. List your business for free
  2. A real person verifies it before it goes live
  3. Get discovered through deals, events and reels

For customers

  1. Search by town, category or rating
  2. Read reviews from verified visits
  3. Request quotes or Ask Lokari

SoulScript

A journal for the things you can’t post.

Some days you can’t find the words. So SoulScript lets you say it, snap it or film it instead. Everything stays locked behind your PIN or fingerprint. No followers. No likes. Just you, and a timeline that shows you how far you’ve come.

  • 16moods with intensity tracking
  • 4media types: text, photo, video, voice
  • 100k+users the architecture is built to handle
  • Quick emotional check-in
  • Multi-modal journaling
  • PIN & biometric vault
  • Colour-coded mood timeline
  • Guided prompts & breathing exercises
  • Crisis resources & “On this day”
