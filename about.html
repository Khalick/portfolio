Story

From Maranda
to market

  1. Foundation

    Maranda High School

    Studied at one of Kenya’s most demanding national schools, where I learned discipline and how to compete with the best. Maranda is a boys’ national school in Bondo, Siaya County, with more than a century of history and alumni who include former Prime Minister Raila Odinga. It taught me that where you start does not decide how far you go.

  2. Education

    Kirinyaga University

    BSc Computer Science. This is where I wrote my first real code and realised I could build the things I used to only imagine.

  3. 2025

    CLIPS Technical College

    My first big build. I made CLIPS Technical College’s website and the student portal behind it, from the first line of code to the day it went live.

  4. 2025

    Clevers Schools

    Built the website for Clevers Schools in Nairobi, where students now apply and register for exams online.

  5. Founded

    Lokari Africa

    Good businesses were invisible online and customers had nobody to trust. That annoyed me enough to do something about it. Lokari is now live in 57 Kenyan towns.

  6. Founded

    SoulScript

    A journal for the things you can’t post. Private by default, always.

  7. May 2026

    GITEX Kenya, Nairobi

    Walked into one of Africa’s biggest tech and AI shows and made sure people heard the name Lokari.

  8. Sep 2026

    Odoo Experience Africa

    Led the Lokari team in, and told our story to KBC. One of the proudest days so far.

The thesis

“Africa’s economy runs on the business down the road: the mechanic, the food vendor, the salon. They deserve the same tools to be discovered that the biggest companies have.”

Principles

How I build

  1. I.

    Build for how it works here

    Most software is made for places that look nothing like Kerugoya or Bondo. I build for the towns I know.

  2. II.

    Trust before scale

    A real person checks every business on Lokari. It’s slow. I don’t care. One fake listing costs more trust than a hundred real ones earn.

  3. III.

    Free where it matters

    The kibanda down the road needs customers more than any big brand does. That’s why listing on Lokari is free, and it will stay free.

  4. IV.

    Privacy is the default

    What you write in SoulScript is yours. Nobody sees it unless you say so. Simple as that.

  5. V.

    No vanity mechanics

    I want people to use what I build because it helps them. Not because they can’t put their phone down.

  6. VI.

    Show up in the room

    Suit on, badge on, walk in. The best partners I’ll ever meet won’t find me through a screen.
