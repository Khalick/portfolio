The thesis

“Africa’s economy runs on the business down the road: the mechanic, the food vendor, the salon. They deserve the same tools to be discovered that the biggest companies have.”

Principles

How I build

  1. I.

    Build for how it works here

    Most software is designed for markets that look nothing like a Kenyan town. I start from how people here actually buy, sell and live, then build for that.

  2. II.

    Trust before scale

    Every Lokari listing is verified by a person. It’s slower, and it’s the point. A platform is only as good as its weakest listing.

  3. III.

    Free where it matters

    The businesses that most need to be found can least afford to pay for it. Lokari listings are free, and they stay free.

  4. IV.

    Privacy is the default

    SoulScript entries are private unless you choose otherwise. Respecting people means never making them opt out of being exposed.

  5. V.

    No vanity mechanics

    No forced followers, no likes for the sake of likes. I build products people use because they help, not because they can’t stop scrolling.

  6. VI.

    Show up in the room

    The best ideas and partners don’t come from a screen. I go where the ecosystem meets, listen, and bring what I learn back to the product.
