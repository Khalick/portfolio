Things I’ve built

I’m a founder who writes the code. Beyond my own companies, I design, build and ship digital platforms for colleges and schools in Kenya.

  1. 01

    College platform · Nairobi · 2025

    CLIPS Technical College

    The complete digital platform for CLIPS Technical College, a Nairobi college with more than 500 students training in ICT, business, beauty, film, languages and sign language. I built the public website, with pages for every school and course, a downloadable prospectus and an online application form, and a student information system behind it: a REST API, an admin dashboard and a student portal for records, unit registration, promotions and secure logins.

  2. 02

    School website & admissions · Nairobi · 2025

    Clevers Schools

    The website and admissions platform for Clevers Schools, a Nairobi centre for KCSE, Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel IGCSE, and AS and A Levels. Built on Laravel, it brings together programme pages for every curriculum, online applications, exam registration for private candidates, fees, information for international students, online learning and news, with a light and dark theme.
