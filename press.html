Long bio

Peter Agak Odongo is a Kenyan founder and computer engineer who builds for the towns he knows.

He is the founder of Lokari Africa, a B2B2C SaaS marketplace for local commerce. Lokari gives businesses, from food vendors to mechanics, a verified digital presence, and gives customers one trusted place to find what’s nearby. Every business on Lokari is checked by a real person before it goes live, and listing is free. The platform covers 57 towns in Kenya.

Peter also founded SoulScript, a privacy-first emotional journaling platform. It lets people record how they feel through text, photos, video and voice notes, in a private vault with no followers or likes.

Peter writes the code behind his own products. He also built CLIPS Technical College’s website and student portal, and the website for Clevers Schools in Nairobi.

Peter studied at Maranda High School and holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Kirinyaga University. In 2026 he attended GITEX Kenya and AI Everything Kenya in Nairobi, and led the Lokari Africa team at Odoo Experience Africa, where he was interviewed by KBC.
